# Pikachu Powers & Abilities — 2026-08-19

Source: [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon/pikachu)

## Moves

### Charge

Type: electric | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Raises the user’s Special Defense by one stage. User’s Electric moves have doubled power next turn.

### Fling

Type: dark | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: physical

Throws held item at the target; power depends on the item.

### Protect

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Prevents any moves from hitting the user this turn.

### Echoed Voice

Type: normal | Power: 40 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: special

Power increases by 100% for each consecutive use by any friendly Pokémon, to a maximum of 200.

### Thunderbolt

Type: electric | Power: 90 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: special

Has a chance to paralyze the target.
