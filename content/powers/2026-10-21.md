## Moves

### Charge Beam

Type: electric | Power: 50 | Accuracy: 90 | PP: 10 | Class: special

Has a chance to raise the user’s Special Attack by one stage.

### Slam

Type: normal | Power: 80 | Accuracy: 75 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

Inflicts regular damage with no additional effect.

### Snore

Type: normal | Power: 50 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: special

Has a chance to make the target flinch. Only works if the user is sleeping.

### Return

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

Power increases with happiness, up to a maximum of 102.

### Play Rough

Type: fairy | Power: 90 | Accuracy: 90 | PP: 10 | Class: physical

Has a chance to lower the target’s Attack by one stage.
