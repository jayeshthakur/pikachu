## Moves

### Snore

Type: normal | Power: 50 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: special

Has a chance to make the target flinch. Only works if the user is sleeping.

### Calm Mind

Type: psychic | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Raises the user’s Special Attack and Special Defense by one stage.

### Signal Beam

Type: bug | Power: 75 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: special

Has a chance to confuse the target.

### Bide

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: physical

User waits for two turns, then hits back for twice the damage it took.

### Sleep Talk

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Randomly uses one of the user’s other three moves. Only works if the user is sleeping.
