### Bide

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: physical

### Volt Tackle

Type: electric | Power: 120 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: physical

User takes 1/3 the damage inflicted in recoil. Has a chance to paralyze the target.

### Mega Punch

Type: normal | Power: 80 | Accuracy: 85 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

Inflicts regular damage with no additional effect.

### Fake Out

Type: normal | Power: 40 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: physical

Can only be used as the first move after the user enters battle. Causes the target to flinch.
