# Pikachu Powers & Abilities — 2026-10-14

Source: [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon/pikachu)

## Moves

### Eerie Impulse

Type: electric | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: status

Lowers the target’s Special Attack by two stages.

### Snore

Type: normal | Power: 50 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: special

Has a chance to make the target flinch. Only works if the user is sleeping.

### Curse

Type: ghost | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Ghosts pay half their max HP to hurt the target every turn. Others decrease Speed but raise Attack and Defense.

### Agility

Type: psychic | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 30 | Class: status

Raises the user’s Speed by two stages.

### Spark

Type: electric | Power: 65 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

Has a chance to paralyze the target.
