## Moves

### Double Edge

Type: normal | Power: 120 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: physical

User receives 1/3 the damage inflicted in recoil.

### Rising Voltage

Type: electric | Power: 70 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: special

If Electric Terrain is active and the user is grounded, this move has has its power doubled.

### Electro Ball

Type: electric | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: special

Power is higher when the user has greater Speed than the target, up to a maximum of 150.

### Uproar

Type: normal | Power: 90 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: special

Forced to use this move for several turns. Pokémon cannot fall asleep in that time.

### Iron Tail

Type: steel | Power: 100 | Accuracy: 75 | PP: 15 | Class: physical

Has a chance to lower the target’s Defense by one stage.
