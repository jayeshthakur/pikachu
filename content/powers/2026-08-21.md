## Moves

### Sleep Talk

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Randomly uses one of the user’s other three moves. Only works if the user is sleeping.

### Rain Dance

Type: water | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 5 | Class: status

Changes the weather to rain for five turns.

### Sweet Kiss

Type: fairy | Power: None | Accuracy: 75 | PP: 10 | Class: status

Confuses the target.

### Rage

Type: normal | Power: 20 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

If the user is hit after using this move, its Attack rises by one stage.

### Nasty Plot

Type: dark | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Raises the user’s Special Attack by two stages.
