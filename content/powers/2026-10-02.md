# Pikachu Powers & Abilities — 2026-10-02

Source: [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon/pikachu)

## Moves

### Protect

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Prevents any moves from hitting the user this turn.

### Charge

Type: electric | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Raises the user’s Special Defense by one stage. User’s Electric moves have doubled power next turn.

### Electro Ball

Type: electric | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: special

Power is higher when the user has greater Speed than the target, up to a maximum of 150.

### Fake Out

Type: normal | Power: 40 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: physical

Can only be used as the first move after the user enters battle. Causes the target to flinch.

### Skull Bash

Type: normal | Power: 130 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: physical

Raises the user’s Defense by one stage. User charges for one turn before attacking.
