# Pikachu Powers & Abilities — 2026-11-08

Source: [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon/pikachu)

## Moves

### Focus Punch

Type: fighting | Power: 150 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

If the user takes damage before attacking, the attack is canceled.

### Slam

Type: normal | Power: 80 | Accuracy: 75 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

Inflicts regular damage with no additional effect.

### Counter

Type: fighting | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

Inflicts twice the damage the user received from the last physical hit it took.

### Electro Ball

Type: electric | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: special

Power is higher when the user has greater Speed than the target, up to a maximum of 150.

### Wish

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

User will recover half its max HP at the end of the next turn.
