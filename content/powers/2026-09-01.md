## Moves

### Attract

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: status

Target falls in love if it has the opposite gender, and has a 50% chance to refuse attacking the user.

### Substitute

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Transfers 1/4 of the user’s max HP into a doll, protecting the user from further damage or status changes until it breaks.

### Captivate

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: status

Lowers the target’s Special Attack by two stages if it’s the opposite gender.

### Helping Hand

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Ally’s next move inflicts half more damage.

### Toxic

Type: poison | Power: None | Accuracy: 90 | PP: 10 | Class: status

Badly poisons the target, inflicting more damage every turn.
