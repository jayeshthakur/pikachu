# Pikachu Powers & Abilities — 2026-08-14

Source: [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon/pikachu)

## Moves

### Take Down

Type: normal | Power: 90 | Accuracy: 85 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

User receives 1/4 the damage it inflicts in recoil.

### Attract

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: status

Target falls in love if it has the opposite gender, and has a 50% chance to refuse attacking the user.

### Mud Slap

Type: ground | Power: 20 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: special

Has a chance to lower the target’s accuracy by one stage.

### Grass Knot

Type: grass | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: special

Inflicts more damage to heavier targets, with a maximum of 120 power.

### Spark

Type: electric | Power: 65 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

Has a chance to paralyze the target.
