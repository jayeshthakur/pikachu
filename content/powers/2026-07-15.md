# Pikachu Powers & Abilities — 2026-07-15

Source: [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon/pikachu)

## Moves

### Endure

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Prevents the user’s HP from lowering below 1 this turn.

### Fake Out

Type: normal | Power: 40 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: physical

Can only be used as the first move after the user enters battle. Causes the target to flinch.

### Draining Kiss

Type: fairy | Power: 50 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: special

Drains 75% of the damage inflicted to heal the user.

### Attract

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: status

Target falls in love if it has the opposite gender, and has a 50% chance to refuse attacking the user.

### Bide

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: physical

User waits for two turns, then hits back for twice the damage it took.
