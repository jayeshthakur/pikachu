## Moves

### Curse

Type: ghost | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Ghosts pay half their max HP to hurt the target every turn. Others decrease Speed but raise Attack and Defense.

### Swift

Type: normal | Power: 60 | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: special

### Charge Beam

Type: electric | Power: 50 | Accuracy: 90 | PP: 10 | Class: special

Has a chance to raise the user’s Special Attack by one stage.

### Dynamic Punch

Type: fighting | Power: 100 | Accuracy: 50 | PP: 5 | Class: physical

Has a chance to confuse the target.

### Wild Charge

Type: electric | Power: 90 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: physical
