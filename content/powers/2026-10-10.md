# Pikachu Powers & Abilities — 2026-10-10

Source: [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon/pikachu)

## Moves

### Endeavor

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 5 | Class: physical

Lowers the target’s HP to equal the user’s.

### Bide

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: physical

User waits for two turns, then hits back for twice the damage it took.

### Charge

Type: electric | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Raises the user’s Special Defense by one stage. User’s Electric moves have doubled power next turn.

### Growl

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 40 | Class: status

Lowers the target’s Attack by one stage.

### Thunder Wave

Type: electric | Power: None | Accuracy: 90 | PP: 20 | Class: status

Paralyzes the target.
