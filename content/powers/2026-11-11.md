### Facade

Type: normal | Power: 70 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

Power doubles if user is burned, paralyzed, or poisoned.

### Reflect

Type: psychic | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Reduces damage from physical attacks by half.

### Fake Out

Type: normal | Power: 40 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: physical

Can only be used as the first move after the user enters battle. Causes the target to flinch.

### Encore

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 5 | Class: status

Forces the target to repeat its last used move every turn for 2 to 6 turns.
