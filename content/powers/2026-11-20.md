# Pikachu Powers & Abilities — 2026-11-20

Source: [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon/pikachu)

## Moves

### Substitute

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Transfers 1/4 of the user’s max HP into a doll, protecting the user from further damage or status changes until it breaks.

### Calm Mind

Type: psychic | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Raises the user’s Special Attack and Special Defense by one stage.

### Draining Kiss

Type: fairy | Power: 50 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: special

Drains 75% of the damage inflicted to heal the user.

### Flail

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: physical

Inflicts more damage when the user has less HP remaining, with a maximum of 200 power.

### Swagger

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 85 | PP: 15 | Class: status

Raises the target’s Attack by two stages and confuses the target.
