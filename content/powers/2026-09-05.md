## Moves

### Facade

Type: normal | Power: 70 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

Power doubles if user is burned, paralyzed, or poisoned.

### Double Kick

Type: fighting | Power: 30 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 30 | Class: physical

Hits twice in one turn.

### Substitute

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Transfers 1/4 of the user’s max HP into a doll, protecting the user from further damage or status changes until it breaks.

### Fling

Type: dark | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: physical

Throws held item at the target; power depends on the item.

### Captivate

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: status

Lowers the target’s Special Attack by two stages if it’s the opposite gender.
