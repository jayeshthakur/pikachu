# Pikachu Powers & Abilities — 2026-07-19

Source: [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon/pikachu)

## Moves

### Headbutt

Type: normal | Power: 70 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: physical

Has a chance to make the target flinch.

### Volt Tackle

Type: electric | Power: 120 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: physical

User takes 1/3 the damage inflicted in recoil. Has a chance to paralyze the target.

### Sleep Talk

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Randomly uses one of the user’s other three moves. Only works if the user is sleeping.

### Fake Out

Type: normal | Power: 40 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: physical

Can only be used as the first move after the user enters battle. Causes the target to flinch.

### Thief

Type: dark | Power: 60 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 25 | Class: physical

Takes the target’s item.
