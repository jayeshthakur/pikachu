## Moves

### Wild Charge

Type: electric | Power: 90 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 15 | Class: physical

User receives 1/4 the damage it inflicts in recoil.

### Confide

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Lowers the target’s Special Attack by one stage.

### Charge

Type: electric | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Raises the user’s Special Defense by one stage. User’s Electric moves have doubled power next turn.

### Encore

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 5 | Class: status

Forces the target to repeat its last used move every turn for 2 to 6 turns.

### Agility

Type: psychic | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 30 | Class: status
