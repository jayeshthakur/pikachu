## Moves

### Tickle

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: status

Lowers the target’s Attack and Defense by one stage.

### Confide

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Lowers the target’s Special Attack by one stage.

### Charm

Type: fairy | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: status

Lowers the target’s Attack by two stages.

### Curse

Type: ghost | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 10 | Class: status

Ghosts pay half their max HP to hurt the target every turn. Others decrease Speed but raise Attack and Defense.

### Draining Kiss

Type: fairy | Power: 50 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: special

Drains 75% of the damage inflicted to heal the user.
