# Pikachu Powers & Abilities — 2026-09-02

Source: [PokeAPI](https://pokeapi.co/api/v2/pokemon/pikachu)

## Moves

### Pay Day

Type: normal | Power: 40 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: physical

Scatters money on the ground worth five times the user’s level.

### Charge

Type: electric | Power: None | Accuracy: None | PP: 20 | Class: status

Raises the user’s Special Defense by one stage. User’s Electric moves have doubled power next turn.

### Fake Out

Type: normal | Power: 40 | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 10 | Class: physical

Can only be used as the first move after the user enters battle. Causes the target to flinch.

### Iron Tail

Type: steel | Power: 100 | Accuracy: 75 | PP: 15 | Class: physical

Has a chance to lower the target’s Defense by one stage.

### Flash

Type: normal | Power: None | Accuracy: 100 | PP: 20 | Class: status

Lowers the target’s accuracy by one stage.
